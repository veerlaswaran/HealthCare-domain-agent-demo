Telemedicine consultations are available for General Medicine, Dermatology, and Pediatrics; Cardiology and Orthopedics require in-person visits for first-time patients due to the need for physical examination and diagnostic equipment. Patients must have a stable internet connection and a device with a front-facing camera to be eligible for a video consultation. Follow-up telemedicine sessions are permissible for all specialties if the patient has had at least one prior in-person visit with the same doctor within the last 6 months. Telemedicine is not permitted for emergency presentations, patients below 5 years of age, or cases that require a physical procedure or investigation. Prescriptions issued during telemedicine sessions are digitally signed and legally valid under the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020.
