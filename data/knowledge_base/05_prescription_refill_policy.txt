source: prescription_refill_policy

Prescription refills for chronic-condition medications (such as antihypertensives, antidiabetics, and thyroid drugs) can be requested through the Practo app or portal without scheduling a new consultation, provided the original prescription was issued by a Practo-registered doctor within the last 90 days. Controlled substances, Schedule H1 drugs, and narcotic analgesics cannot be refilled remotely and require an in-person visit with the prescribing physician. The refill request is reviewed by the doctor within 24 hours on working days; the patient is notified of approval or rejection via SMS. Approved refill prescriptions are sent digitally and are also available for collection at the clinic pharmacy. Patients who require a change in dosage or have developed new symptoms must book a fresh consultation rather than using the refill pathway.
