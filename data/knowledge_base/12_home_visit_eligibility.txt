Home visits are available for General Medicine and Pediatrics within a 10-kilometre radius of the clinic, subject to doctor availability, and must be booked at least 3 hours in advance. Patients eligible for home visits include the elderly (age 65+), patients with certified mobility impairments, and post-surgical patients within 2 weeks of discharge who have been referred by a Practo doctor. A home-visit surcharge of ₹500 is added to the standard consultation fee to cover travel and equipment costs. Cardiology, Dermatology, and Orthopedics home visits are not available because these specialties require in-clinic diagnostic equipment. All home-visit requests are confirmed by the clinic coordinator via phone at least 1 hour before the scheduled visit time.
