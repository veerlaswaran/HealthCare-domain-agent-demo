Patients must cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before the scheduled appointment time to avoid a late-cancellation fee. Cancellations made within 4 hours of the appointment attract a cancellation charge of ₹100, which is deducted from any prepaid amount before refund. Rescheduling is free of charge if requested more than 4 hours in advance and can be done up to twice per appointment without penalty. If a patient does not show up without prior notice, the slot is marked as No-Show and any prepaid consultation fee is forfeited. Refunds for eligible cancellations are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method.
