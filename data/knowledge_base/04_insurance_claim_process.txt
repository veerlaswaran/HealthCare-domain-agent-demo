Practo clinics are empanelled with most major Indian health-insurance providers; patients should confirm their insurer's tie-up status at the front desk before the consultation. To initiate a cashless claim, the patient must present the original insurance card, a valid photo ID, and the pre-authorisation letter from the insurer at the time of check-in. For reimbursement claims, patients pay the full fee upfront and collect an itemised receipt, doctor's prescription, and discharge summary, which must be submitted to the insurer within 30 days. The clinic's insurance helpdesk, reachable at extension 102, assists patients with form-filling and document verification on working days between 9 AM and 5 PM. Claims for cosmetic procedures, dental treatments, and non-prescription supplements are not covered regardless of the insurance plan.
