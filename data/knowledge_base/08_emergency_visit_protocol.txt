Patients experiencing a medical emergency should proceed directly to the clinic's emergency bay, which operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including public holidays. The triage nurse will assess severity using a 3-tier system: Critical (immediate treatment), Urgent (seen within 30 minutes), and Non-Urgent (seen within 2 hours). For life-threatening conditions such as chest pain, stroke symptoms, severe breathing difficulty, or loss of consciousness, the clinic will simultaneously initiate treatment and arrange transfer to a partner hospital equipped with an ICU if required. Emergency consultations are billed separately from scheduled appointments and are not eligible for follow-up discounts. Patients or their attendants must provide identity and insurance details as soon as the patient is stable; treatment is not delayed pending documentation.
