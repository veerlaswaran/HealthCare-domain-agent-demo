All patient health records, consultation notes, lab results, and billing information are stored in encrypted form and accessed only by authorised clinical and administrative staff. Practo complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and does not share identifiable patient data with third parties without explicit written consent, except where mandated by law (e.g., notifiable disease reporting). Patients have the right to access their complete medical records at any time by submitting a request through the portal; records are provided within 5 working days. Data is retained for a minimum of 7 years from the date of the last consultation, in line with Indian medical-records regulations. Any suspected data breach is reported to the patient and to the relevant regulatory authority within 72 hours of detection.
