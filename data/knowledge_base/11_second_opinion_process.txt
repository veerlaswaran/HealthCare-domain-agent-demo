source: second_opinion_process

Patients who wish to obtain a second opinion can request one through the Practo portal by selecting "Second Opinion" at the time of booking and uploading all relevant reports, prescriptions, and imaging results. A senior consultant from the same or a related specialty will review the case and provide a written second-opinion report within 48 hours for non-urgent cases. Second-opinion consultations are priced at the standard specialist rate for the relevant specialty; no additional administrative fee is charged. If the second-opinion doctor recommends a different treatment plan, the patient can switch their care to that doctor within the same Practo network at no rebooking charge. Second opinions are not available for emergency or inpatient cases through this channel.
